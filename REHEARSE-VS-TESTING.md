# Rehearse vs. Established Testing Frameworks

**Why not just use pytest, `go test`, Jest, or Cucumber?** Short answer: Rehearse isn't a unit-test rival — it's an acceptance-evidence layer, organized around acceptance criteria in the spec rather than functions in the code.
